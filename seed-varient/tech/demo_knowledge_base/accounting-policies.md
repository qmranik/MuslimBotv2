# Quazi-Tech Accounting Policies

## Fiscal Year
April 1 – March 31 (Bangladesh standard).

## Currency
BDT (Bangladeshi Taka). All prices include VAT where applicable.

## Chart of Accounts

ERPNext's standard Bangladesh chart of accounts is used with these key accounts:

| Account | Type | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| Sales | Income | Revenue from product sales |
| Cost of Goods Sold | Expense | Purchase cost of sold items |
| Debtors | Receivable | Customer outstanding balances |
| Creditors | Payable | Supplier outstanding balances |
| Cash | Asset | Physical cash at store |
| Bank (bKash/Nagad) | Asset | Mobile banking receipts |
| Bank (Standard) | Asset | Bank account transactions |

## Payment Terms

- **Walk-in customers**: Payment at time of sale (Cash, bKash, card).
- **Corporate clients**: Net 30 (payment due within 30 days of invoice).
- **Government/university**: Net 45.
- Overdue invoices accrue no interest but trigger reminder at 7, 14, and 30 days.

## Discount Policy

- Staff discount: 5% on all items (requires manager approval).
- Bulk discount: 3% for 5+ units, 5% for 10+ units of same item.
- Promotional discounts: set per campaign, applied as "Pricing Rule" in ERPNext.
- Maximum discount without manager approval: 5%.

## Expense Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Rent | Store rent, warehouse rent |
| Utilities | Electricity, internet, phone |
| Salaries | Staff payroll |
| Marketing | Facebook ads, banners, flyers |
| Logistics | Delivery charges, courier fees |
| Maintenance | Store repairs, equipment maintenance |

## Tax

- VAT: 5% on electronics (standard rate for computer accessories in Bangladesh).
- Income tax: Filed annually per NBR guidelines.
- All invoices must include the company TIN and BIN numbers.
