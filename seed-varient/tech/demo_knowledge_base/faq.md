# Quazi-Tech Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering & Payment

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept Cash, bKash, Nagad, bank transfer (BEFTN/NPSB), and credit/debit cards. Corporate clients can arrange credit terms.

**Q: Can I get a bulk discount?**
Yes. Orders of 5+ identical units get 3% off. 10+ units get 5% off. Corporate/institutional orders over BDT 500,000 receive custom pricing — contact our sales team.

**Q: Do you offer EMI?**
EMI is available on select credit cards (0% EMI for 3-6 months on purchases over BDT 20,000). Ask at the counter for eligible cards.

**Q: Can I place an order over the phone or chat?**
Yes. Our AI assistant can help you place orders directly. Provide your name and the items you want, and we'll prepare your order for pickup or delivery.

## Products & Warranty

**Q: Are your products original/authentic?**
All products are sourced from authorized distributors with full manufacturer warranty. We do not sell refurbished items unless explicitly labeled.

**Q: What is the warranty policy?**
Warranty varies by product (12-60 months). Laptops: 12-36 months. Components (RAM, SSD, GPU): 36-60 months. Peripherals: 12 months. Warranty is handled through the manufacturer's authorized service center.

**Q: Can I upgrade my laptop's RAM or SSD?**
Most business laptops have soldered RAM (not upgradeable). NVMe SSDs are upgradeable on almost all models. We offer upgrade services at the store.

**Q: Do you build custom PCs?**
Yes! Select your components and we assemble, test, and install your OS for free. Custom build assembly takes 1-2 business days. We also have pre-configured builds available.

## Delivery & Shipping

**Q: Do you deliver?**
Yes. Dhaka metro: BDT 100 flat rate, free for orders over BDT 10,000. Outside Dhaka: via Sundarban/SA Paribahan courier, BDT 200-500 depending on weight. Delivery takes 1-3 days in Dhaka, 3-7 days outside.

**Q: Can I pick up in-store?**
Absolutely. Orders placed online or via chat are typically ready for pickup within 2 hours during business hours.

## Returns & Exchange

**Q: What is your return policy?**
Unopened products can be returned within 7 days for a full refund. Opened products can be exchanged within 3 days if defective (DOA — Dead on Arrival). Software issues are not covered under returns.

**Q: My product arrived damaged, what do I do?**
Contact us immediately with photos. We'll arrange a replacement or refund within 48 hours.

## Account & Support

**Q: How do I check my order status?**
Ask our AI assistant with your order ID or name. Corporate clients can also check via their account dashboard.

**Q: I need a formal quotation for my organization.**
Our AI assistant can generate a quotation. Provide the items and quantities, and we'll create a formal quote with our company letterhead.

**Q: Do you offer on-site IT support?**
Yes, for corporate clients with service agreements. Individual support is available at our store.
