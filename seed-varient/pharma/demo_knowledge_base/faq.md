# Quazi-Pharma Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering & Payment

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
Cash, bKash, Nagad, and card (debit/credit). Corporate clients and clinics can arrange credit (baki) terms.

**Q: Can I order medicines over the phone or chat?**
Yes. Tell the AI assistant what you need and it will prepare your order. Pick up at the counter or request delivery.

**Q: Do you deliver?**
Yes. Local delivery within 2km is free for orders over BDT 500. Beyond that, BDT 30 flat delivery charge. Orders placed before 6 PM are delivered same day.

## Medicines

**Q: Do I need a prescription?**
Antibiotics and controlled medicines require a valid prescription from a registered doctor. OTC medicines (painkillers, antacids, vitamins) can be sold without prescription.

**Q: Can I buy just a few tablets instead of a full strip?**
Yes, for most medicines we can sell individual tablets. The price is calculated per tablet from the strip price.

**Q: What if my medicine is out of stock?**
The assistant will suggest an equivalent generic alternative. For example, if Sergel is out, Seclo (same omeprazole 20mg) may be available.

**Q: Do you check for drug interactions?**
Our staff pharmacist reviews all prescriptions. If you're taking multiple medications, please mention them so we can flag potential interactions.

## Returns

**Q: Can I return medicines?**
Opened medicine strips cannot be returned. Unopened, undamaged items can be returned within 48 hours with receipt. Temperature-sensitive items (insulin, eye drops) are non-returnable.

## Grocery & FMCG

**Q: Do you sell groceries?**
Yes. We carry daily essentials: rice, lentils, spices, cooking oil, dairy, beverages, soap, shampoo, and toothpaste.

**Q: Are grocery items discounted for bulk purchase?**
We offer 5% discount on grocery purchases over BDT 2,000.

## Account & Credit

**Q: Can I buy on credit (baki)?**
Regular individual customers can maintain a tab up to BDT 2,000. Clinics and businesses can arrange higher credit limits with management approval. Outstanding balances are reviewed weekly.

**Q: How do I check my outstanding balance?**
Ask the AI assistant "amar baki koto?" or "check my balance" with your name.
