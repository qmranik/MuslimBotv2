# Quazi-Pharma Product Catalog

## Company Overview
Quazi-Pharma is a neighborhood pharmacy and convenience store in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We sell prescription medicines, OTC drugs, vitamins, personal care items, and daily grocery essentials.

**Location:** Mirpur-10, Dhaka
**Hours:** Saturday–Thursday, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM; Friday 3:00 PM – 10:00 PM
**Currency:** BDT (Bangladeshi Taka)
