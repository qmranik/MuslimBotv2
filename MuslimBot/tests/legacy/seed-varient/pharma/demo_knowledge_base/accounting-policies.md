# Quazi-Pharma Accounting Policies

## Fiscal Year
July 1 – June 30 (Bangladesh standard).

## Currency
BDT (Bangladeshi Taka). Medicine prices are MRP (Maximum Retail Price) set by DGDA.

## Payment Terms

- **Walk-in customers**: Payment at time of sale (Cash, bKash, Nagad, card).
- **Regular customers (baki/credit)**: Maximum BDT 2,000 outstanding per individual.
- **Clinics & businesses**: Net 15 (payment within 15 days of invoice).
- **Overdue policy**: Reminders at 7 and 14 days. Credit suspended after 21 days overdue.

## Discount Policy

- No discount on prescription medicines (MRP regulated by DGDA).
- Grocery items: 5% discount on purchases over BDT 2,000.
- Bulk clinic orders (>BDT 10,000): negotiate 3-5% with management.
- Staff discount: 10% on personal care/grocery, not on medicines.

## Medicine Pricing

All medicine prices follow the Drug Administration's MRP.
The pharmacy margin is built into the MRP — typically 15-20% above distributor price.
Prices cannot be raised above MRP. Selling below MRP is allowed but rarely done.

## Key Accounts

| Account | Type | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| Medicine Sales | Income | Revenue from medicine sales |
| Grocery Sales | Income | Revenue from FMCG/grocery |
| Cost of Goods Sold | Expense | Purchase cost of sold items |
| Debtors | Receivable | Customer outstanding (baki) |
| Creditors | Payable | Distributor outstanding |
| Cash | Asset | Cash at counter |
| bKash/Nagad | Asset | Mobile money receipts |

## Expense Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Rent | Store rent |
| Utilities | Electricity (including refrigerator), internet |
| Salaries | Pharmacist, helpers |
| Distributor payments | Medicine and FMCG purchases |
| Delivery | Local delivery costs |
| Expired stock write-off | Medicine past expiry date |

## Tax & Compliance

- Pharmacy license must be renewed annually with DGDA.
- Maintain a poison register and controlled substance log.
- VAT is generally not applicable on essential medicines in Bangladesh.
- VAT on FMCG/grocery items: per NBR schedule (varies by item).
- Income tax filed annually per NBR guidelines.
